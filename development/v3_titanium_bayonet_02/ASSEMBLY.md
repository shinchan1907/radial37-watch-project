# Proposed assembly — validate on a prototype first

1. Inspect all finished dimensions, remove burrs/powder, clean and verify component identity. Do not attempt assembly with unfinished stock preforms.
2. Mount the finished single-piece dial to the actual 2035 using its integral feet. Verify seating and center thickness. Fit the qualified polymer holder and antirotation key.
3. Rear-load the dial/movement/holder into the case, keeping 12 o'clock aligned with the key. The dial is captured from above by the case bezel. Install and size the stem/crown using its actual manufacturer's method.
4. Set the selected hands from the front through the bezel opening. Check their full rotation, mutual clearance and clearance to the unused seconds arbor. Install the matched crystal/gasket only after checking the actual inner dome at every hand radius.
5. Install the approved seal and three qualified preload pads on the back support region. Candidate locked pad space is 0.60 mm, temporarily 0.50 mm during the 0.10 mm release push. Measure force and movement restraint; do not substitute arbitrary foam.
6. Align the three entry tabs. Push the back 0.10 mm above its locked height, turn 90 degrees to the terminal pockets, then let it settle into the retention drop. Measure torque/retention and confirm repeatable closure with the actual seals/pads. Do not force dry titanium sliding surfaces.
7. To open, apply the validated axial push before reversing 90 degrees, then withdraw through the entry slots. Nominal CAD motion is sampled; it does not establish acceptable operating force or resistance to accidental opening.
8. Fit selected spring bars/strap; verify full tip engagement, clearance and pull strength. Check wear, skin comfort, debris and repeated bayonet cycling. Water resistance requires a separate specification and pressure test.

This sequence and its force/handling limits remain proposed. A failed fit must lead to measurement and revision rather than forcing, sanding a seal fit blindly or modifying the movement.
