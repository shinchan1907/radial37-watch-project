STATUS: NOT FOR MANUFACTURING OR ORDERING

User brief: 37 mm outside diameter, light/slim/inexpensive quartz movement,
domed crystal, radial metallic dial inspired by the provided images, matching
caseback, eventual JLC3DP stainless steel or titanium manufacture.

Created in Blender through the official Blender MCP bridge.
watch_study.blend contains a separate study scene and retains the startup scene.
The existing Blender scenes were not deleted.

CANDIDATE COMPONENTS / RESEARCH
Miyota 2035 standard quartz: manufacturer specifies 3.15 mm height, 3 hands,
and 3-year battery life. Candidate only, not yet fitted in this model.
https://miyotamovement.com/product/2035/
step.parts search 'Miyota 2035' returned no CAD models. No vendor movement
geometry is represented by the study.

Cousins G53073: listing specifies diameter 33.00, H2.50 x T1.30, domed sapphire
with flat bottom. Candidate envelope only; availability, edge geometry and
gasket selection must be verified before the crystal seat is frozen.
https://www.cousinsuk.com/sku/details/gucci/g53073?skuId=2133855

WR Accessories also lists 33 mm double-domed mineral variants, but these are
taller: no-AR listing says 4.6 mm overall and 3.0 mm glass thickness. These
would require a different stack and are NOT interchangeable with this envelope.
https://wraccessories.com/products/33mm-double-domed-wide-chamfer-mineral-crystal-no-ar-coating

JLC3DP lists SLM 316L and Titanium TC4. Its published metal wall guidance is
1.5 mm in this size range, and it recommends tapping small metal threads.
The guide also warns of substantial errors in internal openings. Plan for
machining/reaming gasket seats, spring-bar and crown bores, and seal faces.
https://jlc3dp.com/help/article/3d-printing-design-guideline

VISUAL REFERENCES / NOT PHYSICAL PARTS
The 60-ray dial and 12 major indices are a shader, NOT printable relief yet.
Hands and crown are visual stand-ins, not movement-compatible purchased parts.
The dome is an approximate visual envelope, not vendor optical geometry.
No material performance, final mass, waterproofness or movement fit is claimed.

NEXT DESIGN STAGES
1. Confirm proportions with user.
2. Obtain/check 2035 casing and dial drawings; match inexpensive hands and crown.
3. Design polymer movement carrier with battery and stem-release access.
4. Resolve the thin central dial interface: a solid 1.5 mm printed blank cannot
   simply replace a conventional thin dial. Consider a thin brass center/carrier
   and metal radial chapter ring, or a recessed/machined one-piece dial.
5. Add printable radial relief below the hand sweep and confirm JLC acceptance
   of feature widths. Do not assume fine spokes satisfy general wall guidance.
6. Add crystal gasket/seat, crown tube, spring bars, screwed back retention,
   and gasket. Avoid relying on as-printed microthreads or a snap-fit seal.
7. Export separate finish-machining/print variants and drawings with tolerances;
   run final assembly interference and metal DfAM checks.
