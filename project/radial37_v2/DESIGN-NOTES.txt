RADIAL 37 V2 — SCULPTED DEVELOPMENT / NOT FOR METAL ORDER

This revision addresses the supplied Carter Hurd Prototype B photographs:
https://altapowderdog.wixsite.com/my-site/watches

Reference findings:
- Prototype B uses a compact movement to allow deep peripheral dial arches.
- The original illusion uses a high-domed acrylic crystal and edge magnification.
- The author reports five services rejected the sixty-marker design; specialized
  DMLS engineering support was required. This is not evidence of JLC acceptance.
- Image-derived radii, cross sections, wall thickness and lug curves in this
  adaptation are inferred, not measurements of the original watch.

WHAT CHANGED
- Sixty actual curved dial webs with open slots, not a radial graphic shader.
  Twelve hour webs are broader and dip less than the 48 intervening minute webs.
- An 18.6 mm raised center medallion with a 1.3 mm center through-hole.
- Dial OD 32.4 mm, vertical envelope 3.9 mm; ring and webs are one connected mesh.
- A tapered/flared 37 mm case with lower, curved lugs and an 18 mm strap gap.
- 44 mm overall lug span; case shell body is 5.25 mm high, lugs extend below it.
- Provisional 1 mm spring-bar drillings and 1.5 mm crown-tube drilling.
- Matching stepped, removable-back development with four clearance bores and
  counterbores. No mating threaded bosses or gasket have been released.
- Real fluting on the crown and tapered hands with rectangular red tips.
- Leather straps, stitching, hands, crown, screws and crystal are appearance
  references. They are not approved manufacturing files or purchased-part CAD.

CRYSTAL
The retained 33 mm low-dome sapphire envelope is approximate:
flat underside z=6.55, rim top z=7.85, apex z=9.05 mm.
Lowest caseback face is z=-0.30: approximate overall envelope is 9.35 mm.
The crystal is hidden in the geometry-review hero so the arches can be assessed.
The low sapphire dome will not reproduce the original high-acrylic optics exactly.
Do not freeze a crystal seat from this envelope; obtain real dimensions and gasket.

MOVEMENT
Miyota 2035 was the initial candidate. A smaller two-hand Miyota 5Y20 is now
worth evaluating because it leaves more peripheral dial space. Manufacturer:
https://miyotamovement.com/product/5Y20/
Published movement height 2.54 mm, 2 hands, four-year battery life.
Supplier listing for budget context (not a purchase or a verified local price):
https://www.ofrei.com/page1386.html
step.parts search for Miyota 5Y20 returned no model. No physical movement CAD
has been incorporated. The 1.5 mm center medallion still needs a dial-interface
solution; it cannot be installed as a conventional thin dial without redesign.

MANUFACTURING LIMITS
Minute web nominal width 0.62 mm; vertical thickness 1.05 mm; fine gaps near
the inner ends can be approximately 0.10 mm. Outer dial ring width is 0.65 mm.
These do NOT meet JLC's general 1.5 mm SLM wall guidance. Do not upload these
STLs expecting an approved steel/titanium order. A wider-rib/fewer-slot variant,
casting, or written vendor acceptance of fine features is needed.
The nominal main case-ring wall was increased to at least 1.55 mm before edge
easing; local lugs, drillings and counterbores still require DfAM review.
JLC guideline: https://jlc3dp.com/help/article/3d-printing-design-guideline

VALIDATION AND REMAINING WORK
geometry-checks.json records Blender mesh dimensions, positive volume,
non-manifold edges and connected-component counts for the three exported parts.
All dimensions are mm; STL files are unitless and must be imported as mm.
Only case_v2.stl, arched_dial_v2.stl and caseback_v2.stl are exported.
These are development meshes, not a finalized kit.
Movement carrier, hand fit, dial mounting, exact stem center, crown sealing,
spring-bar retention, back screw engagement and back gasket remain unresolved.
No assembled watch water resistance or final material strength is claimed.
STEP generation remains unavailable because Windows Application Control blocks
the installed CAD kernel. Blender-native geometry was used through Blender MCP.
The earlier cadgen snapshot browser launch failed; this revision is visually
reviewed using Blender renders of the assembly, dial and back, plus CAD Viewer.

EXPORTED-FILE AUDIT
Reimported each saved STL and checked it independently. All three have positive
volume, one connected component, zero non-manifold edges, and mm dimensions
matching the authored model. Sub-micron boolean slivers were removed at a
0.0001 mm vertex-merge tolerance before triangulation/export. The initial case
STL had tiny four-face edges despite the source check; this cleanup corrected
the exported artifact. Caseback counterbores were moved inward to avoid open
notches at the outside rim. These checks do not establish material printability,
assembly fit or sealing.
